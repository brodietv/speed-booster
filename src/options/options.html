Privacy

Speed Booster never sends your data anywhere. There are no analytics, no accounts, no ads and no remote code. Settings and prompts are stored only in your browser. The only network requests it makes go to chatgpt.com, from your own browser tab and signed in as you — the same requests ChatGPT itself makes (for example to export a chat).

How speed mode works

ChatGPT downloads a whole conversation and builds every message on the page, which is what makes long chats crawl. Speed Booster steps in before that happens and hands ChatGPT only the most recent messages. With ChatGPT's newer page-by-page loading, it pauses older pages once you have enough on screen. Older messages are one click away with Load more, the outline lists every prompt, and search covers the entire chat.

Questions

Does ChatGPT forget the hidden messages?

No. Trimming only changes what your browser shows. The full conversation stays on OpenAI's servers and the model still sees all of it.

Why do exports include hidden messages?

Exports fetch the complete conversation fresh from ChatGPT, so nothing is left out — even messages that were never loaded.

Something looks broken after a ChatGPT update.

Turn Speed Booster off with the switch in the toolbar popup to get stock ChatGPT back instantly, and check for an update. Every feature is designed to fail safe, so ChatGPT keeps working even if a feature stops.

How accurate is the token count?

It's an estimate (about 4 characters per token for English). Use it to spot chats that are getting long enough for ChatGPT to lose early details.
